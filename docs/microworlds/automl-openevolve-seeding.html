Briefing · how to read this world

You are watching a model registry grow a third challenger

TRIDENT-Val scores property collateral with a champion/challenger registry. Two models were hand-picked. ADR-0018 asked: can the third one be searched for — and what should the search start from?

EVOLVE-BLOCKThe only mutable thing in the world: the build_model() function. The LLM rewrites it, generation after generation.
The evaluator (frozen)Every candidate is scored on the same 80/20 holdout split and metrics (MAPE, R²) as the champion. It can never be edited by the search.
MAP-Elites archiveNot just "keep the best" — keep the best per niche (code complexity × structural distance). Diversity is stored, not discarded.
IslandsTwo semi-isolated populations evolve in parallel; the best programs migrate across at gen 10. Guards against monoculture.
The LLM (Claude Code CLI)The mutation operator. It sees the parent program, top/diverse programs from the archive, and the evaluator's feedback artifacts — then proposes a diff.
The seedThe program in the block at generation 0. This world's whole question: seed with an AutoML-tuned winner, or with something simple and different?

Controls: scrub the tape at the bottom (click any segment), or use ←/→ and space. Swap the seed cartridge in the header at any time. Hover dots, cells, and chips for details.

Honesty note: nothing trains in your browser — this is a scripted simulation. The simple-seed run is written to land exactly where the real recorded run landed (model/evolved_v1: MAPE 7.82%, R² 0.9415, 15 iterations). The AutoML-seed run is a plausible hypothetical acted out from ADR-0018's reasoning, so you can feel the trade-off it describes.

Two seeds, one budget: where did each land?

"Whether to seed with a strong baseline (e.g. an AutoML-selected model) depends on which goal is active, and the two goals want opposite seeds… Optimizing for raw accuracy: seed with the AutoML result… Optimizing for structural diversity: seed simply."docs/adrs/0018-openevolve-searched-challenger.md

The middle ground the ADR flags as worth trying: don't seed with the AutoML program's code at all — feed its score into the evaluator's artifacts as a calibration target. The search then knows what "good" looks like without inheriting AutoML's architectural bias.
